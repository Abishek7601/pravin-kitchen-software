Formula Calculation

Overview and verification of measurement sizing, pricing multipliers, and quotation calculation rules applied across the system.

Primary Dimension Formula

Measurement & SQ.FT Calculation

Applied to standard modular items (wardrobes, kitchen cabinets, overhead units) where Length, Width, and Height dimensions are specified in inches.

SQ.FT = (Length + Width) × Height / 144
  • Length & Width: Horizontal dimension components (inches).
  • Height: Vertical dimension component (inches).
  • Divisor (144): Converts total square inches into square feet.
Item Pricing Formula

Material & Rate Application

Calculates line-item amounts by multiplying calculated square footage with the unit rate resolved from the active Material, Material Option, and Pricing Mode.

Item Amount = Calculated SQ.FT × Unit Rate
  • Unit Rate: Resolved from Division, Material Option, & Pricing Mode (Economy / Standard / Premium).
  • Offer Price: Optional manual override per item or room when applicable.
Subtotal Calculation

Room & Item Subtotal

Subtotal = Σ(Item Amounts)

Aggregates all active items across configured rooms. Discount amount is subtracted from the taxable base.

GST Calculation

Tax Computation

GST = Taxable Amount × (GST% / 100)

Calculated on the net taxable amount (including other charges, accessories, and travel charge).

Grand Total

Quotation Grand Total

Grand Total = Subtotal + GST

Final payable amount presented on customer PDF quotations, WhatsApp notifications, and administrative dashboards.
